One world, one identity.

A unified dark visual language for the interactive résumé — the rolling-cube game and the static résumé now share a single palette, type system, and component set. Built for the two people who read it: the engineer who wants to play, and the recruiter who wants to scan.

Rendering & reference

The quality bar the UI is tuned to sit inside.

Quality targets

Atmosphere
Near-black stage (#07140E) with cool fill light — the glowing board is the only hero.
Tone mapping
ACES Filmic at 1.1 exposure for cinematic, non-clipping highlights.
Materials
PBR tiles: emissive neon body + brushed-metal trim; soft PCF shadows for depth.
UI overlay
Glass cards (blur 14px) with accent-tinted bloom, so popups feel lit by the same world.
Motion
0.5s easing cycles; reduced-motion respected; emissive pulse signals interactivity.
live three.js — neon player cube on a platform tile
